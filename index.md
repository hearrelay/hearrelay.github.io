# HearRelay

**Sound Monitor for Baby & Noise Check**

HearRelay is a privacy-first audio monitoring app for iPhone and iPad. It lets you listen in real time to sounds picked up by a device's microphone — on the same device, or on another Apple device sharing your Apple ID and Wi-Fi network.

- Baby monitoring from another room
- Locating the source of a noise in your home
- No cloud, no analytics, no ads — everything stays on your local network
